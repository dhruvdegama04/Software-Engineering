Write the Bootstrap class combination you would apply to the article column div, and explain

1. How Bootstrap's Grid Class System Works
12-Column Layout: Bootstrap divides each row into 12 virtual vertical columns.

Mobile-First Breakpoints: Grid classes use breakpoint abbreviations based on screen width:

xs (<576px) – Extra small / phones (default)

sm (≥576px) – Small / landscape phones

md (≥768px) – Medium / tablets

lg (≥992px) – Large / desktops

xl (≥1200px) – Extra large / widescreen

xxl (≥1400px) – Ultra large screens

Inheritance: Breakpoint classes apply to that screen size and everything larger unless overridden by a larger breakpoint class.

2. Typical Bootstrap Class Combination for an Article Column
A standard responsive layout for an article column (e.g., full width on mobile, 8 out of 12 columns on medium screens and above) uses this combination:
**--HTML--**
<div class="col-12 col-md-8">
  <!-- Article Content Here -->
</div>

3. Explanation of Each Class in the Combination
--col-12

Controls: Base / Extra-Small Screen Width (Mobile-First)

Role: Sets the width of the div to 12 out of 12 columns (100% width) on mobile devices and narrow screens (<768px). This ensures the main article stacks vertically above or below sidebars for easy reading on phones.

--col-md-8

Controls: Medium Screens and Above (Tablets, Laptops, Desktops)

Role: At the md breakpoint (≥768px) and larger, this overrides col-12 and sets the article width to 8 out of 12 columns (66.66% width). This leaves the remaining 4 columns available for a sidebar (col-md-4) next to the article on wider screens.
