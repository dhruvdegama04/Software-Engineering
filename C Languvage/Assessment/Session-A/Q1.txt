2. Two Risks of Choosing the Wrong Methodology
Massive Rework and Sunk Costs (Using Waterfall for Uncertain Scope):

If a team chooses Waterfall for a project with changing user needs or unclear requirements, they risk spending months building according to initial assumptions. When testing or client review happens late in the cycle, required changes can force extensive, costly architectural redesigns.

Scope Creep and Budget Overruns (Using Agile for Fixed-Price/Regulated Work):

If a team uses Agile on a strict fixed-bid or highly regulated project (e.g., medical software or aviation), constant iteration without strict baseline controls can lead to unapproved feature bloat, delayed compliance validation, and missed non-negotiable hard deadlines.
